# Requisition and Approval Process

Synthetic, team-created document describing how the shop moves from "we are running low" to "goods on the shelf".

## Step 1: Check stock
Staff or the AI assistant check which items are at or below their reorder point.

## Step 2: Prepare a draft requisition
A draft lists each item, the recommended quantity, the chosen supplier, the unit price and the line total, plus the estimated budget. Each draft has a unique ID, the date and the name of the person who created it. The AI assistant can prepare drafts but cannot approve them.

## Step 3: Review and edit
The approver reviews the draft. The approver may change a quantity before approving; the original suggestion and the reason for the change are recorded.

## Step 4: Approve or reject
The approver approves or rejects the draft. A rejection must include a reason. Approval is recorded with the approver's name and the time.

## Step 5: Order and pay
After approval, the owner places the order with the supplier and pays according to the Payments and Budget Policy. Ordering and payment happen outside the AI system.

## Step 6: Receive goods
Staff count the delivered goods, compare them with the approved requisition and report any damage within 48 hours.
