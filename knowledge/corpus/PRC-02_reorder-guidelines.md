# Reorder Guidelines

Synthetic, team-created document explaining how reorder decisions are made.

## Reorder point
Each item has a reorder point. When the stock on hand is at or below the reorder point, the item is "low" and should be considered for restocking.

## How much to order
The recommended quantity covers about one and a half months of normal demand: take the average monthly quantity bought over the purchase history, multiply by 1.5, subtract the current stock and round up. If the supplier has a minimum order quantity, order at least that amount.

## Items without history
New items with no purchase history cannot be calculated. A person must decide the quantity, usually by asking the supplier about typical demand or starting with a small trial order.

## Urgency
When the budget is limited, restock the most urgent items first. An item is more urgent when its stock is a smaller fraction of its reorder point.

## School terms and seasons
Demand rises at the start of each school term (late January to February, late May, and September). Before these peaks, reorder points for exercise books, pens, pencils and rulers are raised by 30%. Reorder points are reviewed at the start of each term.
