# Rules for Using the AI Procurement Assistant

Synthetic, team-created guidance.

## What the assistant can do
The assistant can show stock levels, list low-stock items, compare supplier quotes, calculate recommended reorder quantities, answer questions about past purchases and shop policies, and prepare draft requisitions.

## What the assistant cannot do
The assistant cannot approve requisitions, place orders, contact suppliers, make payments or connect to banks or mobile money. It must refuse such requests.

## Who uses the assistant
The assistant is used by the shop owner or a trusted person they appoint. Anyone with access is trusted, so there are no separate user roles; protect access to the computer or phone it runs on. The same person reviews and approves the drafts the assistant prepares.

## Checking the assistant
All numbers come from the shop's records through calculation tools. If an answer looks wrong, check the draft details and the records before approving. Report mistakes to the AI Engineering Lead.

## Data
Only the shop's own records are used. Do not type customer personal information into the assistant.
