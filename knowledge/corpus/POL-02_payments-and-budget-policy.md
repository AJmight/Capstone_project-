# Payments and Budget Policy

Synthetic, team-created document. Fictional shop: Kiwatule Stationery & Office Supplies.

## Who pays suppliers
Only the owner pays suppliers. Staff and the AI assistant never make payments, never send mobile money and never give bank details to suppliers.

## Payment methods
Suppliers are paid by mobile money from the shop's registered business line or by bank transfer from the shop account. Cash payments above UGX 200,000 are not allowed.

## When to pay
Pay only after the goods have been received, counted and checked against the approved requisition. Prepayment of more than 50% of an order needs the owner's written approval.

## Weekly restock budget
The owner sets a weekly restock budget, normally between UGX 300,000 and UGX 600,000 depending on the season. A restock requisition should stay within that budget. Items that do not fit are deferred to the next week or approved separately by the owner.

## Budget estimates
Draft requisitions show an estimated budget range of plus or minus 10% around the calculated total, because supplier prices change. The approver should check that the upper end of the range is affordable.
