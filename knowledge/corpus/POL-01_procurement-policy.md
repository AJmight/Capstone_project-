# Procurement Policy

Synthetic, team-created document for the SME Procurement Support Agent capstone. Fictional shop: Kiwatule Stationery & Office Supplies, Kampala.

## Purpose
This policy explains how the shop decides what to buy, who may approve purchases and how records are kept. It protects the shop's cash flow and stops over-ordering, especially when suppliers pressure new staff.

## Who may request a purchase
Any staff member may prepare a purchase requisition. The AI Procurement Assistant may prepare a draft requisition, but a draft is only a proposal. No requisition becomes an order until a person with approval authority approves it.

## Approval thresholds
Every requisition needs human approval before any order is placed.
- Up to UGX 500,000: the shop supervisor or the owner may approve.
- Above UGX 500,000 and up to UGX 2,000,000: only the owner may approve.
- Above UGX 2,000,000: only the owner may approve, and a written justification must be attached.
Nobody may approve a requisition they created themselves.

## Quotations
For any item costing more than UGX 100,000 in one requisition, compare at least two written supplier quotations. The cheapest suitable quote should normally be chosen. Choosing a more expensive supplier needs a written reason, for example faster delivery when stock has run out.

## Over-ordering safeguard
A requisition line larger than 200% of the largest quantity ever bought of that item in one month must be justified in writing before approval.

## Records
All requisitions, approvals and rejections are kept for five years. Rejections must state a reason.
