# Guidance: Handling Supplier Pressure

Synthetic, team-created guidance for junior staff.

## The problem
Visiting sales representatives sometimes push staff to buy more than the shop needs, offering "today only" discounts. Over-ordering ties up cash in stock that does not sell.

## What staff should do
- Never agree to an order on the spot. Staff have no authority to place orders.
- Ask the representative to send a written quotation to the owner.
- Check the stock level and reorder point first. If the item is not low, politely decline.
- Use the AI assistant's figures (stock, history, recommended quantity) as evidence in the conversation.
- Report repeated pressure from a supplier to the owner.

## Gifts
Staff may not accept gifts, cash or "commission" from suppliers.
