# Procurement FAQ

Synthetic, team-created frequently asked questions.

## Can the AI assistant approve an order if the owner is away?
No. Approval always needs a person with approval authority. If the owner is away, the supervisor may approve requisitions up to UGX 500,000; larger ones wait for the owner.

## What happens to items that do not fit the weekly budget?
They are deferred to the next week's restock or approved separately by the owner.

## Which supplier do we use for an urgent small order?
Nakawa Stationers, because it delivers fastest and has the lowest minimum order (10 units).

## How long do we keep requisition records?
Five years.

## Who sets reorder points?
The owner, reviewed at the start of each school term.

## What should I do if a delivery is damaged?
Report it within 48 hours with photos and the delivery note, then follow the supplier's returns rule.

## Can I pay a supplier in cash?
Only the owner pays suppliers, and cash payments above UGX 200,000 are not allowed.
