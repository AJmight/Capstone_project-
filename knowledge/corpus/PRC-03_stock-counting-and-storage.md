# Stock Counting and Storage

Synthetic, team-created document.

## Weekly and monthly counts
A quick count of fast-moving items is done every Saturday after closing. A full count of all items is done on the last working day of each month.

## Discrepancies
If the counted stock differs from the recorded stock by more than 5% for any item, the difference is reported to the owner the same day and the record is corrected only after the owner agrees.

## Storage rules
Paper and exercise books are stored off the floor on pallets, away from windows and the roof leak area, to prevent water damage. Electronics such as calculators are kept in the locked cabinet.

## Record backups
Stock and purchase records are kept digitally and backed up daily. The old paper exercise book is no longer the main record, because a single water-damaged book once destroyed months of history.
